AI-integrated technical assessments

Each challenge follows the same path: solve the engineering questions of your own (ID-generated) system, train a model on your own dataset (MATLAB Learner apps, or directly in the browser for first-year courses), and prove in a simulation that it beats the conventional engineering method. Marks, a certificate and a full PDF report are issued at the end.
